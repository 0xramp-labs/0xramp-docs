P2P.me is a global platform designed to help you easily buy, sell, and pay using USDC securely and privately. It supports popular local payment methods like UPI, PIX, QRIS, and your funds remain completely in your control.

### Do I need KYC to use P2P.me?

 ![](https://p2p.me/_astro/accordianPlus.xAb_xxZn.svg) ![](https://p2p.me/_astro/accordianMinus.DkSkdwRZ.svg)

No KYC is required to start using P2P.me. You can buy, sell and pay instantly, up to set limits. For unlocking higher limits on your transactions, you can verify your identity in a privacy-friendly manner by completing ZK-KYC (Zero-Knowledge) methods in the app, which verifies your identity details without ever exposing your data or sharing it to any third party (even P2P.me doesn't get access)

### What are the default limits on P2P.me?
