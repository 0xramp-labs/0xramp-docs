The **Donation** option in the sidebar lets you contribute to the Nepal Relief campaign using crypto.

## How it works

The donation uses a locked SELL flow: your crypto is converted and sent to a canonical Fonepay QR destination that is publicly verifiable. The destination is committed on-chain, so it cannot be silently changed.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Donation">
    Click **Donation** in the sidebar of [0xramp.app](https://www.0xramp.app).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose amount">
    Enter the amount you want to donate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Review and confirm. The flow executes as a standard sell order with the donation destination locked in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  This is a campaign-specific feature. Availability and details may change. The donation destination is a public official address -- it is intentionally transparent.
</Note>
