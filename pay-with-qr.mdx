<Warning>
  Pay with QR is a **pilot feature**. Start with small amounts and expect occasional friction. Report issues via [Telegram](https://t.me/zeroxramp).
</Warning>

Pay with QR lets you pay a local QR bill (Pix, UPI, etc.) with ZEC. You send ZEC, it converts to USDC on Base, and a partner merchant pays the bill's QR in local currency from their own account. Your bank is never involved.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select country and amount">
    Open the **Pay** tab in [0xramp.app](https://www.0xramp.app). Choose your payment country and enter the fiat amount you want to pay. The app shows how much ZEC you need to send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide a refund address">
    Enter a transparent ZEC address for refunds. Use **Fill from NOIR** if you have Noir Wallet connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send ZEC">
    Send the exact ZEC amount shown to the deposit address. This funds the PAY order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track the payment">
    Once your ZEC is confirmed and converted, a partner merchant pays the QR in local currency from their own account. Your bank is not part of this payment. If the order is refunded, the ZEC goes to your transparent refund address, and the refund is public on the Zcash chain. See [Privacy](/privacy).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Only static Pix is supported for now. Dynamic Pix QR codes are blocked. The 12-country ceiling comes from the P2P.me country catalog.
</Note>

For details on how the underlying protocol works, see the [Zcash forum post](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/introducing-0xramp-non-custodial-zec-local-fiat-pix-and-more-for-emerging-markets/57215).
