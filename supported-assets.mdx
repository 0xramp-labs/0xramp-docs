## Address safety

- **Copy-paste your address.** Never type it manually.
- **Match asset and network.** USDC on 0xramp settles on Base. Sending Base USDC to an Ethereum-only wallet will lose funds.
- **Transactions are irreversible.** There is no recovery mechanism.
