The 0xramp buy flow lets you convert Brazilian reais (BRL) into crypto — ZEC, SOL, ETH, USDC, and more — without 0xramp ever holding your funds. You pay via Pix directly from your bank app, and the asset is delivered straight to your wallet address in around 6 minutes. Before you start, make sure you have a connected account or wallet and access to a Pix-enabled bank app.

## Requirements

- A connected **P2P.me smart account** or external wallet (Rabby or Phantom)
- A **Pix-enabled bank app** to complete the payment
- The **destination wallet address** where you want to receive your crypto

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select asset and amount">
    Open [0xramp](https://www.0xramp.app) and make sure you're on the **Buy** tab. Choose your destination chain (for example, Zcash) and the asset you want to receive (for example, ZEC). Then enter the BRL amount you'd like to spend.

    The minimum order is **R$ 30**. The maximum per order is set by P2P.me based on your on-chain reputation and verification level — you'll see your current limit in the UI.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your receiving address">
    Paste the wallet address where you want your crypto delivered. Take a moment to double-check every character — **blockchain transactions are irreversible**, and sending to the wrong address means permanent loss of funds.

    <Warning>
      Always double-check the receiving address before confirming. Crypto sent to the wrong address cannot be recovered.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the quote">
    Before you pay, 0xramp shows you a full breakdown of what you're agreeing to:

    - **Fixed fee** — a flat service fee per order
    - **Asset conversion** — the cost of converting USDC to your target asset via NEAR Intents
    - **Exchange rate spread** — the difference between the mid-market rate and the rate used for your order
    - **Estimated amount you'll receive** — the final crypto amount after all fees

    Review the numbers, then tap **Buy** to confirm the order. Confirming places the order on-chain.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pay via Pix">
    A Pix QR code and/or copy-paste key will appear on screen. Open your bank app and pay the **exact amount shown** within the time limit displayed.

    - You can scan the QR code or copy the Pix key manually.
    - Make sure the payment amount matches exactly — partial payments may cause the order to fail.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for delivery">
    Once your Pix payment is matched by P2P.me, the conversion is processed via NEAR Intents and your crypto is sent to the address you provided.

    **Estimated delivery time: ~6 minutes.**

    You can monitor the order status on-screen. No action is needed on your part after payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The quote and delivery time are estimates. The final amount you receive may vary slightly based on market conditions at the time of settlement.
</Note>

## What if something goes wrong?

If your Pix payment was sent but the crypto never arrived, or if you encounter any other issue with a completed order, visit the [Refunds & Failed Trades](/refunds-failed-trades) page for guidance on next steps.
