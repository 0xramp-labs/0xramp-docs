Buy ZEC, NEAR, SOL, ETH, USDC, and other assets by paying with your local currency. 0xramp never holds your funds.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select asset and amount">
    Open [0xramp.app](https://www.0xramp.app) and make sure you're on the **Buy** tab. Choose your destination chain and asset, then enter the amount in your local currency. Minimum and maximum amounts vary by corridor and are shown in the UI.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your receiving address">
    Paste the wallet address where you want your crypto delivered. Double-check every character -- blockchain transactions are irreversible.

    <Warning>
      For ZEC, use a transparent address (starting with `t`). Shielded addresses are not supported for delivery.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the quote">
    The app shows a full breakdown before you pay: fixed fee, asset conversion cost, exchange rate spread, and the estimated amount you'll receive. Review and confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay via your local rail">
    A payment code appears (Pix QR, UPI ID, etc.). Pay the **exact amount** from your bank app within the time limit shown. Partial payments may cause the order to fail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your crypto">
    Once your payment is matched, the conversion runs and your crypto arrives at the address you provided. Estimated delivery: around 6 minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The quote is an estimate. The final amount may vary slightly based on market conditions at settlement.
</Note>

If something goes wrong, see [Failed Trades](/failed-trades).
