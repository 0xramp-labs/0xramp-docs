## General

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is 0xramp a bank?">
    No. 0xramp is a non-custodial protocol interface. It does not hold deposits, offer interest, issue credit, or provide banking services. Funds flow through on-chain smart contracts, not through 0xramp.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is 0xramp the same as P2P.me?">
    No. P2P.me is the infrastructure layer (identity, KYC, merchant matching, USDC escrow on Base). 0xramp is the product built on top of it. When you use 0xramp, you interact with P2P.me infrastructure under the hood.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What countries are supported?">
    Stable: BRL (Pix), INR (UPI), IDR (QRIS), ARS (Alias), VES (Pago Movil). Alpha: COP, NGN, ECU, BOB, CUP, PEN, PHP. See [Supported Assets](/supported-assets) for the full table.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does 0xramp charge fees?">
    No. 0xramp charges zero product fees. All fees come from P2P.me and NEAR Intents and are shown in the quote before you confirm. See [Fees & Quotes](/fees-quotes).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Login and wallets

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why do I need to log in?">
    Every order is placed on-chain and signed by your account. That same address holds your P2P.me reputation and trade limits. You sign in with the P2P.me account (smart account): gas-free, no manual signatures. 0xramp never holds your keys or payment details.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use my own wallet?">
    Yes. Rabby (EVM chains) and Phantom (Solana) are supported. Your keys stay in your wallet -- 0xramp only reads your public address. See [Getting Started](/getting-started).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does 0xramp store my private key?">
    Never. Your keys remain in your wallet or P2P.me's smart account infrastructure. 0xramp only reads your public address.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if I lose access to my P2P.me account?">
    Account recovery is handled by P2P.me, not by 0xramp. Contact P2P.me support directly. If you prefer full control over recovery, use a self-custody wallet (Rabby or Phantom).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Limits

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is my buy or sell limit low?">
    Limits come from P2P.me's on-chain reputation for the account you connected, not from 0xramp. New accounts often start with a small sell allowance and a buy limit of zero until verification or history improves. Open **Account and limits** in the app to see your current caps. See [Limits & Verification](/limits-verification).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are limits the same across all P2P.me apps?">
    Yes. Your P2P.me reputation and limits are tied to your wallet address, not to any specific app. They follow you across the ecosystem.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Referrals

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do referrals work?">
    Generate a link, share it, and earn a percentage of protocol fees from your invitees' orders. Rewards accrue in USDC on-chain. See [Referrals](/referrals) for the full guide.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does generating a referral link cost anything?">
    No. It's an off-chain signature -- free, no gas.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I got 'your account already has a registered referrer'">
    Each account can only be referred once, permanently. This cannot be changed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Failed trades

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My trade failed or is stuck. What happens to my money?">
    0xramp does not take custody of your funds. If a leg cannot complete, assets are returned to the refund address you set when you started the order (or released according to the on-chain / NEAR status). See [Failed Trades](/failed-trades) for step-by-step guidance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will I get an automatic refund?">
    No. Because 0xramp is non-custodial, there is no central fund pool for instant refunds. Resolution depends on what stage your order reached. Contact [support on Telegram](https://t.me/zeroxramp) with your order ID.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Privacy

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are my transactions private?">
    Partially. On-chain transactions on Base, NEAR, and Zcash (transparent) are publicly visible. P2P.me ZK-KYC protects your identity data -- only a cryptographic proof is used on-chain, not your personal details. After receiving ZEC, shielding is your wallet's job. See [Privacy](/privacy) for the full picture.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Pay with QR

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Pay with QR?">
    A pilot feature that lets you fund a merchant QR payment using ZEC. You deposit ZEC, it converts to USDC, and a partner provides a fresh merchant QR for you to pay. See [Pay with QR](/pay-with-qr).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
