Sell ZEC, NEAR, SOL, ETH, USDC, and other assets. Send crypto to a deposit address and receive local fiat to your bank or payment app.

## How selling works

Selling is a two-leg process:

1. Your asset is swapped to USDC on Base via NEAR Intents.
2. A P2P.me merchant pays you in local fiat from the escrowed USDC.

If the merchant leg fails, the USDC stays in your P2P.me account on Base. You can withdraw it or use it for another trade.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select asset and amount">
    Open [0xramp.app](https://www.0xramp.app) and switch to the **Sell** tab. Choose the asset and enter the amount. Minimum and maximum amounts are shown in the UI.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your refund address">
    Provide a transparent ZEC address (or the equivalent for your asset's chain). This is where funds return if the merchant leg fails. For ZEC, you can use the **Fill from NOIR** button if you have Noir Wallet connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your fiat key">
    Enter the payment key or bank account where you want to receive fiat (Pix key, UPI ID, Alias, etc.). Double-check it -- 0xramp cannot redirect a payment once sent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the quote">
    Review the fiat amount you'll receive, the fee breakdown, and the exchange rate. Confirm to place the order on-chain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send crypto to the deposit address">
    The app shows a deposit address and exact amount. Send that precise amount from your wallet.

    <Warning>
      Send only the exact asset and amount shown. Sending the wrong asset or amount may result in a failed trade. Do not send from an exchange that may alter the transaction.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive fiat">
    Once your deposit is confirmed on-chain, the merchant releases the fiat payment to your key. Funds appear in your bank or payment app shortly after.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If something goes wrong, see [Failed Trades](/failed-trades).
