The 0xramp sell flow lets you convert crypto into fiat currencies and receive the funds directly to your bank account or payments app — no bank linking required on our end. You send the crypto to a deposit address shown in the app, and once it's confirmed on-chain, P2P.me releases the fiat payment straight to you. Before you start, make sure your wallet holds the asset you want to sell and that you have a valid payment app key ready.

## Requirements

- A connected wallet (P2P.me smart account, Rabby, or Phantom) **holding the asset you want to sell**
- A valid **fiat payment key or bank account**.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select asset and amount">
    Open [0xramp](https://www.0xramp.app) and switch to the **Sell** tab. Choose the asset you'd like to sell — ZEC, NEAR, SOL, ETH, BTC, USDC, or another supported asset — and enter the amount you want to convert to fiat.

    The minimum order is **$6.00** equivalent. Your maximum sell amount is set by your P2P.me on-chain reputation and verification level.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your fiat key">
    Enter the payment key or bank account where you want to receive your fiat.

    Double-check that the payment key is correct — 0xramp does not store your credentials and cannot redirect a payment once it has been sent.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the quote">
    Before sending any crypto, review the full breakdown:

    - **Fiat amount you'll receive** — after fees and the current exchange rate
    - **Fee breakdown** — fixed fee, conversion costs, and rate spread

    Confirm the quote when you're ready to proceed. This places the order on-chain.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send crypto to the shown address">
    The app will display a **deposit address** and the **exact amount** to send. Open your wallet and send that precise amount to that address.

    <Warning>
      Send only the exact asset and amount shown. Sending the wrong asset or a different amount may result in a failed trade. See the [Refunds & Failed Trades](/refunds-failed-trades) page if this happens.
    </Warning>

    - Do **not** send from an exchange or third-party custodian that may alter the transaction.
    - On-chain confirmations are required before the fiat release is triggered — do not close the app until you see the order status update.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Receive fiat">
    Once your crypto deposit is confirmed on-chain, P2P.me verifies the transaction and releases the fiat payment to your key or bank account automatically.

    The selected fiat currency should appear in your bank/payment app balance shortly after on-chain confirmation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Fiat payments may take a few minutes to appear in your bank app after the on-chain confirmation, depending on your bank's processing time.
</Note>
