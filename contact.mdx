## Telegram

Join the 0xramp community and get support on Telegram: [t.me/zeroxramp](https://t.me/zeroxramp)

## What to have ready

When contacting support, include:

- **Order ID** -- visible on the [Activity](/activity) page. This is the most important piece of information.
- **Wallet address** -- the address associated with your order.
- **Transaction references** -- payment reference from your bank app (for buys) or on-chain transaction hash (for sells).
- **Description** -- what happened and at which step.
