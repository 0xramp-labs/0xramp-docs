Share your referral link and earn a percentage of the protocol fees from every order your invitees place. Rewards accrue in USDC on-chain.

{/* SCREENSHOT: The Refer and Earn page showing 'Generate referral link' button, 'Reward to Claim: 0.00 USDC', and 'Your Referrals: 0 accounts' section. */}

## For the referrer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check eligibility">
    Open [0xramp.app](https://www.0xramp.app) and go to **Invites** in the sidebar. You need an account limit of **$150+** (same criterion as app.p2p.me). Below that, the panel shows the requirement -- raise your limit via [verification](/limits-verification) to unlock referrals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate your link">
    Click **Generate referral link**. Your wallet asks for one signature -- off-chain, free, no gas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share">
    Copy or share the link. One link serves unlimited invitees. The signature authorizes "whoever arrives through this link," not a specific person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Earn and claim">
    The panel lists your referrals and accrued reward: a percentage of the protocol fees from your invitees' orders, accumulating in USDC on-chain. The exact percentage is read on-chain and may change with protocol configuration. Click **Claim reward** to transfer accrued USDC to your account (gas sponsored on the embedded account).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## For the invitee

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the link">
    The invite is captured and survives reload/login redirects.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect or create an account">
    P2P.me login creates the smart account instantly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the referral">
    A **"You received an invite"** prompt appears. Click **Accept referral**. Your account signs a sponsored transaction -- the referral is recorded on-chain and permanent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rules

- One referrer per account, permanently. Cannot be changed later.
- No self-referral.
- Already-referred accounts cannot re-accept.
- The referrer must be eligible ($150+ limit).

## Interoperability

The smart account is shared with [app.p2p.me](https://app.p2p.me). Links from either app work in the other, and rewards accrue to the same on-chain balance.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does generating a link cost anything?">
    No. It's an off-chain signature -- free, no gas.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the link expire?">
    No. It stays valid and reusable indefinitely.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where does the reward come from?">
    A percentage of protocol fees on your invitees' orders, in USDC. The percentage is read on-chain and may change with protocol configuration.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I got 'your account already has a registered referrer'">
    Each account can only be referred once, permanently. This cannot be changed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
