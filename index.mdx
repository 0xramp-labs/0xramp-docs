0xramp is a non-custodial fiat ↔ crypto on/off-ramp built by **0xramp labs**. Buy assets like ZEC, NEAR, SOL, ETH, and USDC by paying with fiat currencies, or sell crypto and receive fiat directly to your bank account or local payment services (Pix, Pago Movil, Alias, UPI, QRIS) — all without 0xramp ever holding your funds or keys.

## Get started in minutes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open 0xramp">
    Go to [0xramp.app](https://www.0xramp.app) in any browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your account">
    Sign in with your **P2P.me account** (gas-sponsored smart account, no seed phrase) or connect an external wallet like Rabby or Phantom.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your order">
    Choose **Buy** or **Sell**, pick your asset, enter an amount, and review the quote — including all fees — before confirming.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete payment">
    For a buy: pay the fiat invoice (for example, Pix QR code) and your crypto arrives in your wallet. For a sell: send the requested crypto and your bank receives the fiat.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **v1.0.0** is live at [0xramp.app](https://www.0xramp.app). Delivery of ZEC is to a transparent address (t-addr) only. Limits and verification are set by P2P.me, not by 0xramp.
</Note>
