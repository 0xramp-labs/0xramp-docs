0xramp lets you move between crypto and local fiat using peer-to-peer payment rails -- Pix, UPI, QRIS, Alias, Pago Movil, and more. Your wallet places the order on-chain. 0xramp never holds your funds or keys.

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open 0xramp">
    Go to [0xramp.app](https://www.0xramp.app).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Sign in with a **P2P.me account** or connect an external wallet (Rabby, Phantom).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trade">
    Choose Buy, Sell, Swap, or Pay with QR. Review the quote, confirm, and complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Powered by **P2P.me** and **NEAR Intents**.
