Every 0xramp transaction moves through three layers: **P2P.me** orchestrates the order and handles fiat payment plus USDC escrow on Base, **NEAR Intents** converts USDC to or from your chosen asset, and **0xramp** ties it together with quotes and status tracking.

## Buy (fiat to crypto)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter amount and address">
    Choose the asset you want (ZEC, NEAR, SOL, etc.), enter your receiving wallet address, and review the quote.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay via your local rail">
    A payment code appears (Pix QR, UPI ID, Alias, etc.). Pay the exact amount from your bank app. The payment goes to a P2P.me merchant. 0xramp never handles your payment credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your crypto">
    P2P.me matches your payment and locks USDC in escrow on Base. NEAR Intents converts that USDC into your asset and delivers it to your wallet. Typically takes around 6 minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sell (crypto to fiat)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter sell amount and fiat key">
    Select the asset and amount to sell, then enter your fiat payment key (Pix key, UPI ID, Alias, etc.).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your crypto">
    The app shows a deposit address and exact amount. Send from your wallet. This is a two-leg process: your asset is first swapped to USDC on Base via NEAR Intents, then a P2P.me merchant pays you in fiat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive fiat">
    Once your deposit is confirmed on-chain, the merchant releases the fiat payment to your key. Funds appear in your bank or payment app shortly after.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pay with QR (pilot)

Fund a merchant QR payment using ZEC. You deposit ZEC, it converts to USDC, and a PAY order is created. The partner provides a fresh merchant QR for you to pay. See [Pay with QR](/pay-with-qr) for details.

<Note>
  All orders are placed on-chain by your wallet or P2P.me smart account. 0xramp has no custody of your funds at any point.
</Note>
