**v1.0.0** is the current production release.

## v1.0.0 — Production

- Organic app shell: buy/sell, swap, activity, account/limits, invites, help
- Dual identity on both legs: P2P.me smart account or Rabby / Phantom
- Fiat-first quotes; in-app receipts
- Stable corridors: BRL, INR, IDR, ARS, VEN
- Alpha (start small): COP, NGN, ECU, BOB, CUP, PEN, PHP
- ZEC default; transparent t-addr only
- 0xramp product fee remains zero

## v0.9.0 / v0.9.1 — Open beta (historical)

Public beta on 2026-08-20. Buy/sell against the first stable rails, P2P.me identity, NEAR Intents conversion. Superseded by v1.0.0.
