The **Activity** page shows every order you've placed on 0xramp -- buys, sells, swaps, and open orders.

{/* SCREENSHOT: The Activity page showing a list of past orders with type badges (SELL, SWAP, BUY), amounts, dates, order IDs, status badges (Transaction completed, Closed, Delivered), and Receipt buttons, with filter tabs at the top. */}

## Filters

Use the tabs at the top to filter:

- **All** -- every order
- **Buys** -- fiat to crypto orders
- **Sells** -- crypto to fiat orders
- **Swaps** -- NEAR Intents asset swaps
- **Open** -- orders still in progress

## Order statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Transaction completed | Order settled successfully |
| Delivered | Swap output received |
| Refunded | Swap failed, funds returned |
| Closed | Order expired or was cancelled |

## Receipts

Every order has a **Receipt** button. Click it to see the full breakdown: amounts, fees, exchange rate, timestamps, and on-chain references. Keep receipts for your records.

## Pagination

Orders are paginated. Use the arrows at the bottom to navigate between pages.
