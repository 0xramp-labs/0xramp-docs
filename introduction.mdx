0xramp is a product by 0xramp Labs that lets you buy and sell crypto using fiat currencies via local payment services from different countries (Pix, Pago Movil, Alias, UPI, QRIS) — without ever giving up custody of your funds or keys. It is not a bank, not a custodian, and not P2P.me. Think of it as the interface layer that brings together payment infrastructure, on-chain escrow, and cross-asset swaps into a single, straightforward experience.

## Key Facts

- **Non-custodial by design.** 0xramp never holds your funds, private keys, or bank credentials at any point. Orders are placed directly on-chain by your wallet.
- **Powered by P2P.me rails.** Identity verification, KYC, trading limits, fiat payment matching, and USDC escrow on Base are all handled by P2P.me's infrastructure.
- **Asset conversion via NEAR Intents.** Once your fiat hits the P2P.me escrow, NEAR Intents handles the swap between Base USDC and your chosen asset — seamlessly and on-chain.
- **Supported corridors:** BRL via Pix (Brazil), VES via Pago Movil (Venezuela), ARS via Alias (Argentina), INR via UPI (India), and IDR via QRIS (Indonesia).
- **Available assets.** You can buy or sell ZEC, NEAR, SOL, BTC, ETH, USDC, and any asset supported in the NEAR Intents catalog.

<Note>
  **0xramp is not P2P.me.** P2P.me is the infrastructure layer — it owns identity, KYC, Pix matching, limits, and USDC escrow. 0xramp is the product experience built on top of it.
</Note>
