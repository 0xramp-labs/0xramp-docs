0xramp is a non-custodial fiat and crypto on/off-ramp built by **0xramp labs**. Buy assets like ZEC, NEAR, SOL, ETH, and USDC by paying with your local currency, or sell crypto and receive fiat directly to your bank account or payment app.

Your wallet places the order on-chain. 0xramp never holds your funds, keys, or bank credentials.

## How it works

Three layers work together:

- **P2P.me** handles identity, KYC, trading limits, fiat payment matching, and USDC escrow on Base.
- **NEAR Intents** converts USDC to or from your chosen asset on-chain.
- **0xramp** provides the interface, live quotes, and order tracking. It does not touch your funds.

## Key facts

- **Non-custodial.** 0xramp never holds your funds, private keys, or bank credentials.
- **12 corridors.** BRL (Pix), INR (UPI), IDR (QRIS), ARS (Alias), VES (Pago Movil) are stable. COP, NGN, ECU, BOB, CUP, PEN, PHP are in alpha.
- **Zcash-first.** ZEC is the default asset. Delivery is to transparent addresses (t-addr) only.
- **Zero product fees.** 0xramp charges nothing. All fees come from P2P.me and NEAR Intents.

<Note>
  0xramp is not P2P.me. P2P.me is the infrastructure layer that owns identity, KYC, merchant matching, and USDC escrow. 0xramp is the product built on top of it.
</Note>
